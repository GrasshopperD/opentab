**Title:** OpenTab Global

**Tagline:** Fund everyday purchases across borders. Pay without mobile data. Settle on Stellar.

**What problem it solves**: Cross-border digital money is not automatically usable for households facing connectivity, distance or banking barriers. Some recipients need a practical way to pay participating neighborhood merchants directly.

**How it works**: A sender funds a family allowance via Soroban. Recipients authorize exact checkout requests by local QR signing. Connected merchants verify through Stellar Testnet and receive on-chain settlement.
