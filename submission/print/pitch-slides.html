Blockchain Innovation Challenge · Problem Space 3

OpenTab Global

Family support, spendable at the corner shop, approved on a phone with no data, settled on Stellar.

The problem

The money arrives. Then it has to become groceries.

  • Remittances are often collected as cash at a pickup counter first.
  • Many recipients have a phone but unreliable or costly mobile data.
  • Senders can't earmark support for essentials.
  • Small shops extend informal credit and carry the risk.
The solution

A shared family balance, spendable at local shops

Why Stellar

The rules live in a Soroban contract, not our server

  • Each approval binds network, contract, token, family, shop, terminal, invoice, amount, price and expiry.
  • Each invoice can be paid once; replays are refused.
  • One balance across many shops; two shops can never overspend it together.
  • Native Ed25519 verification: no account needed for the shopper.
  • Stellar Asset Contracts hold and pay a dollar token.
  • Low fees, ~5 s finality, anchor ecosystem for future cash-out.
